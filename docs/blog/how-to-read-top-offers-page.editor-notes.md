Check claim 19 against this list. "Eight considered, six used" counts the six definition pages plus the two blocked help pages. If you count differently, change the sentence.

## Dropped as unverifiable

- That CJ or ShareASale quote EPC per 100 clicks. Only third-party pages said so. The FAQ says "some networks are said to" and tells the reader to check. Remove that FAQ sentence if you want zero unsourced statements.
- Digistore24's own marketplace KPI definitions (earnings per sale, cart conversion, cancellation rate). The help page was blocked.
- The formula "APV x conversion rate = EPC" as a ClickBank statement. A search snippet attributed it to ClickBank, but the page I opened did not contain it. The article presents the relationship as arithmetic, not as a ClickBank quote.
- Whether either monthly page carries an earnings disclaimer. The reader tool reported none, but absence is easy to miss, so I cut the claim.

## Conflicts found between sources

- Gravity window: ClickBank's help center says "the past 90 days", its February 2026 blog post says "a 12-week rolling period". Both are reported with attribution.
- EPC basis: ClickBank's glossary says "Revenue", its help center says "net commission". Both are reported.
